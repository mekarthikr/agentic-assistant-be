# Claims Guide

## Filing a Claim

To submit a claim:

1. Login to the portal.
2. Open Claims.
3. Select Create New Claim.
4. Upload supporting documents.
5. Submit the claim.

---

## Required Documents

- Death Certificate
- Government ID
- Policy Number
- Claim Form

---

## Processing Time

Most claims are processed within 7–10 business days after all required documents are received.
