# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I update my beneficiary?

Navigate to Profile > Beneficiary Details and submit the required information.

---

## How can I download my policy document?

Open the Policies page and select Download Policy Document.

---

## Can I change my premium payment frequency?

Yes.

Monthly, quarterly, semi-annual and annual payment options are available.

---

## How do I contact customer support?

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
